# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (short)

Our goal is to provide a welcoming and respectful community for contributors.

Standards
- Be respectful and considerate in all interactions.
- Use inclusive language and avoid personal attacks.
- Report unacceptable behavior to the repository maintainers.

Enforcement
- The maintainers may remove or block contributors who do not follow this code of conduct.

Contact
- To report incidents, open an issue addressed to the maintainers or email the project owner.
